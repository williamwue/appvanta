# AppVanta community conduct

AppVanta contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful, technically constructive and safe to participate in.

Expected conduct includes discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people, accepting correction, respecting privacy, and giving clear attribution. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, sexualized conduct and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to enforce these rules. Enforcement decisions should state the relevant behavior, avoid exposing reporters or device data, and apply proportionate action. Good-faith security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and remain private.

For conduct in GitHub-hosted project spaces, use [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). AppVanta currently has no private project-specific conduct reporting channel. Do not post sensitive details publicly.
